INT. YOUR MAC – ALWAYS

Privacy

Cue collects nothing about you. Your scripts stay on your Mac.

Last updated 15 October 2026

This policy covers Cue, the screenwriting app for Mac, made by Abhinav Prabhakar, and this website.

What Cue collects

Nothing. Cue has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don’t collect, store, sell or share any information about you or what you write. There are no Cue servers for anything to be sent to.

Your scripts

Everything you write, including notes, sticky notes, pinned images and cover pictures, is saved on your Mac in Cue’s own folder. It isn’t uploaded anywhere. A file leaves Cue only when you export it, as a PDF or Fountain file, to a place you choose. Cue opens other files only when you choose them, for example a PDF you import.

The music player

If you allow it, Cue asks Apple Music or Spotify what’s playing (the title, artist and cover art) so it can show it in the corner, and sends play, pause and skip when you press them. This stays between Cue and the music app on your Mac. For Spotify, the cover art is an image Spotify provides from its own servers, so Cue fetches that picture from Spotify; nothing about you or your writing is sent with it.

The waveform

If you allow it, Cue listens to the sound your Mac is playing so the bars in the corner move with your music. The sound is measured as it plays and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

You can turn either permission off at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security (Automation, and Screen & System Audio Recording).

Downloads and reports

Cue is distributed as a direct download from GitHub Releases, so Apple doesn’t send us crash reports, purchase information or anything else. If you report a problem on GitHub, only what you choose to post is shared, and it is public.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. The light mode you pick is remembered only in your browser, on your device, so the page can open in the same light next time. Like any website, the company that hosts it may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Children

Cue collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If Cue ever starts to collect anything, it will ask you first.

Questions

Ask on GitHub Issues.

Cue is made for writing. It doesn’t read your script, send it anywhere, or use it to train anything.
