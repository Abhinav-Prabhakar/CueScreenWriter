INT. SUPPORT – DAY

Support

Something not working, or a question about Cue? Write to support@cuescreenwriter.com. A real person reads every message.

Cue needs a Mac with macOS 15 Sequoia or later.

Writing

  • Enter and Tab move you between scene headings, action, characters and dialogue. Between paragraphs, the small menu in the margin lets you pick any type.
  • Notes: select some words and press Tab, or right-click them and choose Add Note.
  • Sticky notes: right-click the desk beside the page and choose Add Sticky Note.
  • Fix a paragraph’s type: right-click it and choose Format.

Your scripts

  • Cue saves as you type. There’s nothing to remember.
  • Bring a script in: File › Import Script… reads screenplay PDFs and Fountain files.
  • Send a script out: File › Export PDF… or Export Fountain….
  • Backups: Time Machine backs up your scripts with the rest of your Mac. For a copy you can keep anywhere, export it as Fountain, which any screenwriting app can open.

The music player and waveform

  • The player shows what’s playing in Apple Music or Spotify. The first time, your Mac asks whether Cue may control them; choose Allow.
  • If the player stays empty, open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Automation and turn on Music or Spotify under Cue.
  • If the waveform moves in a gentle pattern instead of with the music, turn Cue on under System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording.
  • To quiet Cue’s own sounds (keys, startup, ambience), use Cue › Settings › Sound. Your music keeps playing.

Testing Cue

If you’re testing Cue through TestFlight, use Send Beta Feedback in the TestFlight app, with a screenshot if you can. If you used the earlier beta from a download, quit it before opening the TestFlight version: your scripts move across by themselves the first time.

Privacy
